How it moves your icons

ShapeDesk works out a spot for every icon on your desktop, then asks Finder to move it there.

Shapes are curves

Each shape is a math curve, like the classic heart. ShapeDesk spaces your icons along it, clear of the menu bar and Dock. With multiple displays, it uses the screen whose menu bar you clicked.

Words are strokes

Words are drawn in a thin font, then thinned to one-pixel lines. Icons go on the ends and corners of each stroke first, because that's what makes a letter readable, then fill the gaps evenly.

Finder does the moving

ShapeDesk asks Finder to move each icon, the same way an AppleScript would. It sends 14 eased steps without waiting on each reply, so 40 icons glide into place in about a second.

A place for every file.

AI Sort is part of ShapeDesk Pro. It sorts loose files on your Desktop or in the folder open in Finder into eight categories. Obvious files sort locally; AI checks the rest. Undo puts your files back.

Obvious files. Zero AI checks.

With “Sort obvious files on this Mac” on by default, standard-named screenshots and recordings, documents, source code, archives and camera photos go straight to their folders. Ambiguous files still go to AI. Both use the same safe moves and undo; you can change this in Settings.

A high bar for AI moves

When a file needs AI, it gets one of eight folders and a confidence score. It moves only above 80%. Lower scores and errors leave it in place. Obvious files use local rules instead.

Names, not contents

AI Sort sends a file's name, type, size and dates to ShapeDesk's service, which asks TypeSafe's Jev model to choose a folder. File contents and full paths never leave your Mac, and obvious files, like default-named screenshots, are sorted on your Mac without being sent at all.

Undo, even offline

Undo last sort puts every file back, even after a restart and without a connection. ShapeDesk never overwrites a file, and it leaves files alone while they're open, locked, downloading or just changed.

Right in Finder

Open a folder in Finder, then open AI Sort from the menu bar: ShapeDesk follows that folder. Use Desktop switches back. Or select files from one folder, right-click and choose Services → Sort with ShapeDesk. Only files directly inside the folder are sorted.

See what's using your space.

Storage is part of ShapeDesk Pro. For the folder open in Finder, or your Desktop, it shows what takes the most room, which files are exact copies, and what you haven't opened in months. It all happens on your Mac; no file details leave it.

Biggest first

Sizes lists everything in the folder, largest first, with the space each item really takes on disk. A folder's size includes everything inside it, and clicking one shows what's in there.

Copies, set aside

Duplicates finds files with identical contents and lets you choose which copy to keep. The others move into a Duplicates folder beside them. Undo last move puts them back, even without Pro, and is separate from AI Sort's undo.

Not opened lately

Review files in the current folder and installed apps you haven't opened in 30 days, 90 days, 6 months or a year. Running apps are left out. You choose what to move to Trash through Finder, where Put Back works until you empty it.

  2. Open it once

Questions

What can ShapeDesk see on my Mac?

Shapes use the names and positions of your desktop items through Finder; that information stays on your Mac. AI Sort handles obvious files locally by default. For files that need AI, it sends only the name, type, size and dates to ShapeDesk's service and TypeSafe's Jev model, never file contents or full paths. Storage reads sizes and dates and compares contents locally. Update checks contact GitHub for the latest release. Your Pro recovery key stays in your Mac's Keychain.

Is ShapeDesk still free?

Yes. Shapes, words, Reset to grid and Settings are free. AI Sort and Storage need active Pro access: $5 a month for 1,000 AI checks, shared across up to 3 Macs, or a valid Pro coupon. Storage and files sorted locally don't use checks. Undo for sorts and duplicate moves remains available without Pro.

Does Storage remove files automatically?

No. You choose which duplicate copies to move into the Duplicates folder, and Undo last move restores them, with or without Pro. Not Opened lists files and apps for you to review. Move to Trash asks you to confirm and goes through Finder, so Put Back works until you empty the Trash. Nothing is offloaded automatically.

Can AI Sort lose or overwrite my files?

It moves files into eight category folders beside them and never deletes or overwrites a file. Obvious files use local rules; AI decisions require more than 80% confidence. Undo last sort restores the latest sort, even offline. A confidence score isn't a guarantee of the right category.

What counts as an AI check?

One completed classification of one file, whether it moves or stays. Failed checks are free, and obvious files that ShapeDesk sorts on your Mac, like default-named screenshots, don't use one. Your checks reset on the 1st of each month (UTC).

How do I redeem a Pro coupon?

In the app, open AI Sort → Account → Have a coupon?, enter the code and choose Redeem for Pro. No payment card is needed. Each code can be used once per account, and another valid coupon adds days to existing coupon access. Coupons aren't available on an active paid subscription.

How do I use Pro on another Mac?

On the Mac where you subscribed, open AI Sort → Account → Copy recovery key. On the other Mac, open AI Sort → Account → Already subscribed? Restore access and paste the key. One subscription covers up to 3 Macs.

I replaced a Mac and hit the 3-Mac limit

Sign in at shapedesk.space/account with your recovery key and deactivate the Mac you no longer use. Your subscription isn't affected.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Manage billing in the app's Account screen, or sign in at /account and choose Manage billing. Either opens the Stripe portal; canceling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.

Will I get my old layout back?

Reset to grid lines every icon up in a tidy grid, sorted by name, the way Finder arranges a fresh desktop. ShapeDesk doesn't save the exact spots your icons were in before, so take a screenshot first if your layout matters to you. AI Sort's Undo last sort is different: it restores the latest sort exactly, even offline.

Can I open ShapeDesk from the keyboard?

Yes. Press ⌃⌥⌘D in any app, or record your own shortcut in the panel's Settings (the gear). Settings is also where you turn on Open ShapeDesk at login.

Does it work with multiple displays?

Yes. Click ShapeDesk in the menu bar on the display you want to use. Your desktop icons arrange on that screen, with room for its menu bar and Dock.

How do I update ShapeDesk?

Open Settings with the gear, then choose Check now. Automatic checks can show an update link when a newer release is available; they don't download or install anything. You can also download the latest version, quit ShapeDesk and replace the app in Applications.

Why do my icons jump back right away?

Finder's desktop sorting is on. Right-click the desktop, choose Sort By → None, and turn off Use Stacks.

How many icons do I need?

Shapes work with any number. Words read best with about 7 icons per letter, so 40 icons spell a five-letter word well. If icons crowd each other, raise the Size slider.

Does it work with iCloud Desktop & Documents?

Yes. Items that are still syncing may land outside the shape. Pick the shape again once they've finished.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Why isn't it notarized?

Notarization needs a paid Apple developer account. Until then, the full source is on GitHub, so you can read it or build it yourself.

How do I quit or uninstall it?

If you subscribe to Pro, cancel it first in Manage billing (the app's Account screen, or the account page). Then click the grid icon in the menu bar and choose Quit. To uninstall, drag ShapeDesk from Applications to the Trash.
